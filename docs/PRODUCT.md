# SignalPilot V1 — Product dossier

## ICP
TPE/PME locales à forte valeur par client.

## Promise
Mesurer, expliquer et améliorer les signaux qui rendent une entreprise compréhensible et découvrable dans les moteurs et assistants IA.

## Core loop
Scan → Score → Opportunities → Action → Monitor → Re-score.

## V1 scope
1. URL scan
2. business extraction
3. explainable score
4. query plan
5. opportunity engine
6. optional LLM analysis
7. report
8. dashboard shell
9. billing boundary
10. monitoring boundary

## Non-goals
- Promise of ranking
- Fake access to proprietary AI search indexes
- Automatic publishing without authorization
- Invented business facts
- Full agency white-label in V1

## Score
Structure 5%
Business understanding 20%
Offer coverage 20%
Local signals 15%
Authority 15%
AI readiness 25%

This is a diagnostic score created by SignalPilot, not a search-engine score.

## Launch KPI
- scan completion rate
- email/lead capture
- audit purchase rate
- subscription conversion
- activation rate
- 30-day retention
- AI/provider cost per active account
- gross margin
