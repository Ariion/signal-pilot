# 90-day roadmap

## Week 1
Ship scanner, report, pricing page, analytics events.

## Week 2
Persistence, auth, Stripe checkout, email report.

## Week 3
LLM provider, better extraction, query runner abstraction.

## Week 4
WordPress connector and publish-preview workflow.

## Month 2
Google Business Profile integration, review intelligence, competitor history.

## Month 3
Agency workspace, Shopify connector, API, scheduled monitoring.

## Gate criteria
Do not build the next phase unless the previous phase produces evidence:
- 100 scans
- 10 paid reports
- 5 active subscriptions
- measurable activation
